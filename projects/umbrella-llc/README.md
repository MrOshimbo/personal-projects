# Umbrella LLC

## Overview

Explore creating an umbrella limited liability company (LLC) that could provide a shared business structure for multiple ventures over time.

One possible venture discussed so far is organizing and producing paid events for bands. The broader idea is to have a flexible business home for more than one business or revenue-generating project, rather than treating each idea as completely unrelated.

This is an early-stage concept, not a decision to form an LLC.

## Initial concept

- **Umbrella structure:** Investigate whether one parent or umbrella LLC could support multiple ventures.
- **Live music events:** Explore producing paid events for bands, including the business model and practical operating requirements.
- **Future ventures:** Leave room for additional business ideas as they develop.

## Questions to explore

1. What kinds of ventures might belong under the umbrella?
2. Would one LLC with separate DBAs be appropriate, or would separate subsidiary LLCs provide better separation?
3. What liability, insurance, licensing, tax, accounting, and contractual considerations would apply to live music events?
4. What would the event business look like financially—venues, ticketing, artist agreements, promotion, production costs, and revenue sharing?
5. At what point would the idea justify formation costs and ongoing administration?

These are research questions, not legal or tax conclusions. Get advice from qualified legal and tax professionals before choosing or forming a structure.
